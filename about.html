About Our System

Making printing services more accessible and convenient for BIST students.

WHO WE ARE

A Smarter Way to Manage Printing Services

The Online Printing Request and Scheduling System is designed to provide students of BIST with a more organized and convenient printing experience.

This system aims to address delays in printing services by allowing students to submit printing requests online, select preferred pickup schedules, and monitor their requests.

Through this platform, the printing process can become more efficient, accessible, and organized.

🎯

Our Mission

To improve the accessibility and efficiency of printing services through a convenient online platform.

👁️

Our Vision

To create a more organized, responsive, and student-friendly printing environment within BIST.

⚡

Our Goal

To minimize waiting time and improve coordination between students and printing personnel.
